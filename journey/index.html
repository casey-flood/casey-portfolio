Dandy · 2025 – now

Today, I design in the real material.

At Dandy I design operator-facing software for dental manufacturing. I started with dentist-facing ordering and chairside flows, then became the sole product designer on the Design Editor, where lab designers prep scans, design restorations, and check quality across fixed, removable, and implant workflows.

Designing in the production codebase.

  1. Prototype in Storybook with real React components
  2. Iterate with Cursor and Claude
  3. Validate with internal users in real workflows
  4. Ship as production UI. The design system lives in Storybook.

Better tools, faster turnaround, fewer remakes, and a better fit for the people we serve.

Before AI was part of how we worked.

  1. Research and flows in Figma and Miro
  2. High-fidelity designs and prototypes in Figma
  3. Hand off to engineering through Jira tickets
  4. Review builds in bi-weekly QA checks

My Trips · Research in the field

I tested it with real people.

Card sorts on a studio floor showed me how people group the actions of sharing a trip. Then I put the prototype in their hands and watched where they hesitated. The biggest finding: people dropped off when asked to review everything at once, which led to daily review prompts.

  1. Card sorting
  2. In-person usability testing
