Work · Amwell · 2019 – 2024

Virtual urgent care, used by millions.

Amwell is an enterprise telehealth platform that health systems and health plans use to deliver virtual care under their own brand. Over five years I grew from UX Designer to Senior Product Designer, shaping the patient experience and the admin tools that power it.

What Amwell is.

A patient starts a visit in the patient portal. A provider picks it up in the provider portal. Care and follow-ups flow back to the patient. The admin is the link behind both: they configure the whole experience.

Where I fit in.

I climbed from UX Designer to Senior Product Designer.

  1. 2019

    UX Designer

    Journeys

    I owned UX and research for the patient experience on mobile and web: onboarding, enrollment, payment, and the waiting room.

  2. 2021

    Senior UX Designer

    Systems

    I led UX strategy for patient experiences: accessibility, vendor-integrated journeys, the "AI While You Wait" triage flow, and information architecture standards reused across client configurations.

  3. 2023

    Senior Product Designer

    Teams

    I owned design direction across patient, provider, and admin on web and white-label mobile. I mentored designers, standardized components, and redesigned the admin platform with a scalable multi-tier framework.
