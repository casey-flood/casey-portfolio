Work · Dandy · 2025 – now

Designing both sides of a digital dental lab.

Dandy connects dentists, dental designers, and manufacturing through software. I've designed the dentist-facing ordering flows and the designer-facing Design Editor.

Dentist experience

Dentist scans

A 3D scan of the mouth replaces the physical mold.

The scan can come from Dandy’s own Dandy Vision scanner Dandy Vision scanner Dandy’s intraoral scanner, hardware Dandy manufactures. meetdandy.com ↗ , or from third-party scanners like 3Shape Trios 3Shape Trios An intraoral scanner from 3Shape. 3shape.com ↗ and iTero iTero An intraoral scanner from Align Technology. itero.com ↗ .

Dentist experience

Dentist orders

The dentist uses Chairside, Dandy’s ordering software, to view the scans, choose the product, the material, and preferences like fit, aesthetics, and shipping time.

Lab tech (designer) experience

Designer designs

A dental designer (lab tech) receives the order from the dentist through the Dandy Ops portal and designs the restoration in 3D.

Manufacturing and ops

Crown is made

The approved design is manufactured, much of it automated.

Zirconia is milled from a ceramic block, sintered in a furnace, then stained, glazed, and polished. Dandy automates much of it with robotics and, by its own account Dandy crown and bridge lab How Dandy makes crowns and bridges, in Dandy’s words. meetdandy.com ↗ , checks each crown against the original scan with computer vision before it ships.

Dentist and patient experience

Crown is placed

The dentist checks the fit and places the crown.

This used to run through small local labs: physical molds shipped back and forth, with little visibility into the process. Dandy digitized and automated it at scale, for faster turnaround and more volume. Dandy reports up to 50% fewer remakes and first-fit accuracy above 98% Dandy crown and bridge lab Dandy’s published remake and first-fit figures. meetdandy.com ↗ .
